## SwissGrid

**Name:** SwissGrid

**Subtitle:** Swiss grid systems and grid-locked type sizes for Photoshop

**Price:** $30, one-time (Perpetual)

**Categories / tags:** Design, Layout, Typography · grid, grid system, Swiss style, International Typographic Style, layout, guides, baseline grid, typography, poster design, Müller-Brockmann

**Support email:** [support email]
**Help URL:** [site]/index.html#swissgrid
**Privacy policy URL:** [site]/privacy.html
**Terms of service URL:** [site]/terms.html

**Description:**

> Design posters, pages and layouts in the Swiss / International Typographic Style, right inside Photoshop.
>
> SwissGrid builds Müller-Brockmann-style grids from your margins, columns, rows and gutters, in mm, px, in, cm, pt or %. Every grid gets its own color, so a blue field grid and a red baseline grid can sit on the same canvas, and you can stack as many as you like.
>
> **Grids**
> • Margins, columns, rows, widths and gutters in any unit. Switch units and every field converts exactly, with no rounding drift.
> • Each grid is drawn as guides, a locked colored layer, or both.
> • Starter presets: 6×6 square, A3 poster, A4 and Letter with baselines, golden section divisions and a golden textblock. Save, export and import your own.
> • Hide, show or remove any grid. Clear everything in one click.
>
> **Type that locks to the grid**
> • Reads your grid's baseline unit and recommends Caption, Body, Subhead, Heading, Title and Display sizes whose leading lands on whole lines.
> • Shows how many columns body text should span for a comfortable 45–75 character line (Bringhurst).
> • Select text layers and click Apply to set size and leading.
> • Apply matching baseline builds the baseline grid that belongs to your field grid, one line per body leading.
>
> **Clean and safe**
> • Every action is a single Ctrl+Z.
> • Grids are saved inside your .psd, so they're still there when you reopen the file.
>
> **Even better with SwissType:** SwissType reads the grids SwissGrid saves and snaps your text onto the baseline grid in one click.
>
> Grounded in Josef Müller-Brockmann's *Grid Systems in Graphic Design* and Robert Bringhurst's *The Elements of Typographic Style*.
>
> Requires Photoshop 2023 (24.2) or later.

## SwissType

**Name:** SwissType

**Subtitle:** Baseline snap, optical alignment and type scales for Photoshop

**Price:** $15, one-time (Perpetual)

**Categories / tags:** Design, Typography · typography, baseline grid, baseline snap, type scale, optical alignment, measure, line length, text styles, Swiss style, Bringhurst

**Support email:** [support email]
**Help URL:** [site]/index.html#swisstype
**Privacy policy URL:** [site]/privacy.html
**Terms of service URL:** [site]/terms.html

**Description:**

> The typography tools Photoshop never had, in one panel.
>
> **Align: baseline snap.** Select text layers and click once. Each layer moves so its first baseline sits exactly on a line of your baseline grid or on the nearest horizontal guide. Works with point text and paragraph text, and reports how far each layer moved.
>
> **Align: optical edge align.** Aligns the letters themselves, not the text box, to the nearest guide, so a headline's first letter sits exactly on the margin.
>
> **Scale: type scales.** Traditional or Fibonacci (Bringhurst) scales, golden ratio, perfect fifth, perfect fourth or major third, with leading in whole baseline units. Apply sets size and leading, and can snap to the baseline afterward.
>
> **Measure: line-length checker.** Characters per line using Bringhurst's alphabet-length copyfitting, words per line, a rag chart for point text, and the measure or size you'd need for 66 characters.
>
> **Styles: text styles.** Save font, size, leading, tracking, color, case and alignment from any text layer, reapply them anywhere, and export or import them as files.
>
> Every action is a single Ctrl+Z.
>
> **Works on its own, best with SwissGrid.** Grids you apply with SwissGrid are saved inside your document. SwissType finds them automatically, picks the baseline grid, and snaps to it exactly as SwissGrid drew it. Without SwissGrid, snap to your own guides or type in a baseline grid's top and line spacing.
>
> Requires Photoshop 2023 (24.2) or later.
