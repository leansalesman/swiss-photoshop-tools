Privacy

SwissGrid and SwissType don't collect, send or sell any personal information. They work entirely on your computer.

What the plugins don't do

  • They don't connect to the internet. They make no network requests and have no network permission.
  • They don't collect analytics, usage statistics, crash reports or identifiers.
  • They don't read your artwork's pixels or text, except to measure and move the text layers you select when you click a button.
  • We never receive any of your data.

What stays on your computer

  • Settings, presets and text styles are saved in the plugin's own storage on your computer. SwissGrid also remembers which grids you applied to which document, by document name.
  • Grids inside your documents. When you apply a grid, SwissGrid saves a description of it (its name, color and line positions) in the document's metadata, so SwissType can find it and it's still there when you reopen the file. This metadata travels with the .psd if you share it. Clear all grids removes it.
  • Files you choose. Export and Import read or write a preset or style file only at the location you pick.
  • Clipboard. SwissGrid writes a type size to your clipboard only when you Shift-click Apply.
